# Follow or Fade: demo recording guide (60 seconds)

## 1. Prepare (10 minutes before recording)

1. Start the app with `start.bat` and wait until the Live Floor loads.
2. Open a **fresh window**: press **Ctrl+Shift+N** in Chrome, go to `localhost:3000`, pick the nickname `Wassim`. You start clean with $10,000 on the Training Table.
3. Press **F11** for full screen and keep the browser zoom at 100%.
4. On the Live Floor, **place 3 live bets** ($500–$1,000 each): one on **Ride the Whale**, one on **Cigar Lounge (4 hours)** and one on **Espresso (15 min)**. By recording time some will be winning (money-printing face) and some losing (crying face).
5. Sound **on**, volume around 50%.
6. Press **Win+G**, open **Settings → Capturing**, and make sure the microphone is **off** and "Audio to record" is **Game** (so it records the app sounds only).

## 2. Record (press Win+Alt+R to start and stop)

The story is **Train → Practice live → Trade for real on Nansen**. Move the mouse slowly and pause about 1 second on anything important. Record 2–3 minutes; you'll cut it to 60 seconds after.

**Before recording:** play 10–12 quick hands on the Training Table in your private window, so the Skill Report has strengths and leaks to show.

| Time | What to do on screen | Caption to add |
|---|---|---|
| 0–4s | Training Table with a hand dealt: hover the grade chip, the whale stats and the dealer's tells | Learn to read Smart Money, with real Nansen whale trades |
| 4–11s | Click **25%** → **FADE** or **FOLLOW** → roulette → reveal, pause on "the whale closed after …h" | Judged on the whale's real exit, not a random timer |
| 11–17s | Scroll to **What the data said**: point at the tell and the trap | After every hand: which Nansen signal called it |
| 17–23s | Click **Skill Report**: level, edge over the odds, strengths and leaks | Your Skill Report shows what you read well and where you leak |
| 23–31s | **Live Floor**: pause on the **Insider Pick of the Day**, open **Full research**, click **FOLLOW** | Nansen Agent screens every stock on Hyperliquid for insider buying |
| 31–38s | On a stock whale (xyz: …) tap **Insider check**; on any whale pick **Ride the Whale** → **FOLLOW** | Whale vs insiders: same side or not? |
| 38–44s | Your Table: point at the faces, click **CASH OUT** on a winning bet | Cash out anytime. The whale is my exit liquidity |
| 44–50s | Bell → **Send a test alert** → click **Bet on it** | Alerts when a top whale opens a trade, even on Telegram |

If no live bet is winning at 34–40s, cash out a losing one: the "Cashing out before the whale gets rekt" banner works just as well.

## 3. Edit in Clipchamp (free, built into Windows 11)

1. Press the Windows key, type **Clipchamp**, open it and click **Create a new video**.
2. **Import media** → pick your recording (it's in `Videos\Captures`). Drag it onto the timeline.
3. **Cut:** click the timeline where a part should start or end, press **S** to split, select the unwanted piece and press **Delete**. Aim for 55–60 seconds.
4. **Captions:** click **Text** on the left, drag a simple style above the video on the timeline, and type the caption from the table. Put captions at the bottom of the screen, white, about 1 line.
5. **AI voiceover (optional, recommended):** click **Record & create → Text to speech**. Pick English and a calm voice, paste a line from section 4, click **Save to media**, and drag it onto the timeline at the right time.
6. Select the video clip → **Audio** on the right → lower the volume to about **40%** so the voice is clear over the casino sounds.
7. **End card:** Text → a title style → "Built on Nansen API · github.com/tonychdid/follow-or-fade" for the last 3 seconds.
8. **Export** (top right) → **1080p**.
