# Follow or Fade: demo recording guide (60 seconds)

## 1. Prepare (10 minutes before recording)

1. Start the app with `start.bat` and wait until the Live Floor loads.
2. Open a **fresh window**: press **Ctrl+Shift+N** in Chrome, go to `localhost:3000`, pick the nickname `Wassim`. You start clean with $10,000.
3. Press **F11** for full screen and keep the browser zoom at 100%.
4. On the Live Floor, **place 3 live bets** ($500–$1,000 each): two on **Espresso (15 min)** and one on **Champagne (30 min)**. By recording time some will be winning (money-printing face) and some losing (crying face).
5. Sound **on**, volume around 50%.
6. Press **Win+G**, open **Settings → Capturing**, and make sure the microphone is **off** and "Audio to record" is **Game** (so it records the app sounds only).

## 2. Record (press Win+Alt+R to start and stop)

Move the mouse slowly. Pause about 1 second on anything important. It's fine to record 2–3 minutes; you'll cut it to 60 seconds after.

| Time | What to do on screen | Caption to add |
|---|---|---|
| 0–5s | Live Floor at the top, slowly scroll down one card | Follow or Fade: a casino on real Nansen Smart Money trades |
| 5–13s | Hover the whale track record, click **30D**, hover the grade letter | Every whale graded A+ to F from their Nansen 7D/30D track record |
| 13–20s | Pick **Espresso**, click **+500**, click **FOLLOW**, watch it appear on Your Table | Follow or fade the whale. Nansen data sets the odds |
| 20–29s | Point at the faces on Your Table, then click **CASH OUT** on a winning bet (gold button) | Cash out anytime at fair value |
| 29–37s | Click the bell → **Alert rules & channels** → **Send a test alert** → click **Bet on it** | Whale Alerts: top whales opening trades, even on Telegram |
| 37–41s | Click **Trade BTC on Nansen ↗** and show the Nansen page for 2 seconds, then come back | Join the trade on Nansen in one click |
| 41–52s | **The Table** tab → click **25%** chip → **FADE** → roulette → reveal | Replay this week's whale trades and see the next 4 hours |
| 52–57s | **Hall of Fame** tab, then **Share my card** | Climb the Hall of Fame and share your card |
| 57–60s | End card (Clipchamp title) | Built on Nansen API · github.com/tonychdid/follow-or-fade |

If no bet is winning at step 20–29s, cash out a losing one: the "Cashing out before the whale gets rekt" banner works just as well.

## 3. Edit in Clipchamp (free, built into Windows 11)

1. Press the Windows key, type **Clipchamp**, open it and click **Create a new video**.
2. **Import media** → pick your recording (it's in `Videos\Captures`). Drag it onto the timeline.
3. **Cut:** click the timeline where a part should start or end, press **S** to split, select the unwanted piece and press **Delete**. Aim for 55–60 seconds.
4. **Captions:** click **Text** on the left, drag a simple style above the video on the timeline, and type the caption from the table. Put captions at the bottom of the screen, white, about 1 line.
5. **AI voiceover (optional, recommended):** click **Record & create → Text to speech**. Pick English and a calm voice, paste a line from section 4, click **Save to media**, and drag it onto the timeline at the right time.
6. Select the video clip → **Audio** on the right → lower the volume to about **40%** so the voice is clear over the casino sounds.
7. **End card:** Text → a title style → "Built on Nansen API · github.com/tonychdid/follow-or-fade" for the last 3 seconds.
8. **Export** (top right) → **1080p**.
