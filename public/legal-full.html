Risk and the nature of this service

Last updated 18 September 2026

What Follow or Fade is

Follow or Fade is an educational game. It deals you real trades that Nansen has labelled as Smart Money activity on Hyperliquid, and asks you to predict whether following that trader would have made money. You wager play chips with no monetary value. They cannot be bought, sold, redeemed, withdrawn, transferred, or exchanged for anything, and running out of them costs you nothing but a reset.

What it is not

  • It is not investment advice. Nothing on this site is a recommendation to buy, sell, or hold any asset, and nothing here is tailored to your circumstances, objectives, or risk tolerance. The operator is not a licensed investment adviser, broker, or financial institution.
  • It is not a trading venue. You cannot open, hold, or close a real position here. No order ever reaches a real market from this site.
  • It is not a signal service. The odds, probabilities, grades, scores, and alerts are outputs of a statistical model fitted to recent data. They describe what the data said, not what will happen.
  • It is not gambling for money. There is no stake, no entry fee, no payout, and no prize.

The model's numbers

Every probability, payout multiple, trust grade and skill score on this site is produced by a model that is refitted on a rolling window of recent outcomes. It is calibrated on a limited sample, over a short history, in one market regime. It will be wrong, sometimes badly and for long stretches. Performance in this game does not predict real trading results — the game has no fees, no slippage, no funding costs over long holds, no liquidations, no leverage, no position limits, and no emotional cost to losing.

If you decide to trade for real

Perpetual futures are complex leveraged products. This site does not teach futures mechanics, margin, liquidation, or position sizing, does not tell you how to configure anything, and cannot configure anything on any venue for you. Every setting on a trading account belongs to the account holder and is theirs alone to choose.

Anything you do beyond this site is outside it. To the fullest extent permitted by law, the operator accepts no responsibility or liability whatsoever for any decision you take, any position you open or close, any loss or cost you incur, or the availability, accuracy, security or conduct of any third-party service you use. Nothing on this site creates any duty of care, advisory relationship, or obligation of any kind towards you.

Trading leveraged derivatives is not appropriate for most people. You can lose more than you expect and, in some circumstances and venues, more than you deposited. Do not trade with money you cannot afford to lose entirely. Consider taking independent advice from someone licensed in your country. Some jurisdictions restrict or prohibit access to these products altogether — complying with the law where you live is your responsibility.

AI-generated content

The Insider Pick of the Day and the insider checks on stock cards are written by a large language model (Nansen Agent) from Nansen's data. That output is generated automatically and is not reviewed by a person before you see it. It can be incomplete, out of date, or simply wrong, including in the specific figures it quotes. Treat it as a starting point for your own research, not as a statement of fact and not as a recommendation.
